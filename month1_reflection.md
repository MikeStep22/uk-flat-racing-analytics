# Month 1 Reflection

*Written 4 October 2026, at the close of Month 1 — Foundations: vibe coding, domain literacy, first tiny dataset.*

**What was most confusing this month**

Getting to grips with VS Code and its UI, which isn't very intuitive at first — and switching between the Claude Code panel inside VS Code and this Claude session, which aren't the same thing and don't share context. (This turned out to be more than just an annoyance — it's exactly what caused `group1_winners.py` to quietly revert to stale hardcoded data in week 3, fixed and logged in the project plan's Risks section.)

**What clicked**

The power of Claude Code / vibe coding itself. Even knowing this month was deliberately "training wheels" stuff, there was a genuine *aha* moment around the potential for this way of working to change software development — for professional engineers and for non-coders alike.

**What I want Month 2 to answer**

Semantic web concepts and vocabulary (RDF, OWL, SPARQL) still aren't clear, even after a short course taken previously. The goal for month 2 isn't just to tick off the plan's checkpoint (write a SPARQL query from scratch) — it's to come out able to **confidently explain these concepts to someone else**, and why they matter. That's a higher bar than just getting the syntax to run, and worth treating as the real success criterion for the month.
